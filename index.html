How it works

Three steps, and you only do the first one.

1

Add your clients

Enter each client's name, email or phone, and what you need from them: bank statements, receipts, payroll. Or paste in a list.

2

Chasely does the chasing

It sends the reminders on day 0, 3 and 6, on weekday mornings, in the tone you picked. Replies go straight to your inbox.

3

You step in only when needed

Mark items received as they arrive. If a client stays silent after the last reminder, you get an alert. A summary lands every Monday.

Questions bookkeepers ask

Will it message my clients without asking me?

That is your choice. In autopilot, routine reminders send themselves on schedule. In approval mode, every message waits in a queue until you approve it. Start with approval mode if you want to see how it writes first.

Does it replace my accounting software?

No. Chasely sits beside the software you already use. It handles the step before the books: getting the documents from your clients.

What do my clients see?

A short, polite email from "Chasely" listing what is missing, signed with your name. If they reply, it goes to you. Every email has a line they can use to opt out.

Does it read the documents my clients send?

Not yet. Chasely notices when a client sends attachments, but you check and mark items received yourself. Reading documents is on the list for later.

Does it work on WhatsApp?

Email works today. WhatsApp is planned, and founding members will get it first.

How does setup work?

Chasely is in early access, so I set it up with you personally. Send me your client list and what you usually need from each client, and I'll get you running.

Does it give my clients tax or financial advice?

No. It collects documents and sends routine reminders. Anything that needs professional judgment stays with you.

What if it isn't for me?

Ask within 30 days and you get your money back.
